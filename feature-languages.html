How language detection works

01

Auto-detected from what you type

Kavox reads the language of your name and the details you enter, and generates the whole CV in that language automatically. No separate language picker required.

02

25 languages, including RTL

Arabic, English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Ukrainian, Persian, Urdu, Hindi, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Czech, Romanian, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, and Vietnamese, with full right-to-left layout support for Arabic, Persian, and Urdu.

03

Section labels translate too

Headings like Experience, Education, and Skills are shown in the CV's own language, not left in English underneath translated content.

04

One CV, one language

Each generated CV is written natively in a single language end to end. Mixing two languages in the same CV is not currently supported.
